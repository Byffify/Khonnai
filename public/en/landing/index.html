Before you get started

Do I need an account?

Not right now. Open Khonnai and start adding products.

Will my data appear on another device?

Not automatically. Save a backup file on the original device, then open that file in Settings on your other device.

Where is my shop data kept?

It stays on the device you use to open Khonnai. Save a separate backup regularly, especially before clearing device data or switching devices.

Do I need a checkout machine?

No. Use a phone or computer that can open this website.

What should I do first?

Add a few products. Check sizes, colors, prices and quantities against your actual stock, then try a small sale.
